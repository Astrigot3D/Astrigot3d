## Episode Summary

Topology can be tricky, but it's fundamental! We expand on the ideas in our "Practical Guide to 3D Model Topology" tutorial, sharing practical tips and discussing common mistakes.

Tune in to hear about:
* Real-world implications of good vs. bad edge flow.
* Strategies for managing poles effectively.
* When to use Tris and N-gons without causing problems.
* How topology needs change for animation vs. games vs. subdivision modeling.
